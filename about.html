About Custom Wear

Who We Are

At Custom Wear, we turn your ideas into reality, one print, one stitch, and one statement at a time. Every piece we make is designed to represent you.

What We Do

We specialise in personalised apparel for brands, businesses, fitness teams, and creators. Bold streetwear, sleek corporate fits, and custom gym wear, we do it all.

How We Work

Using modern printing techniques and attention to detail, we bring every logo, quote, and name to life with lasting quality and comfort.

Why It Matters

Clothing is more than fabric, it’s identity. That’s why every design starts with your vision and ends with something you’ll be proud to wear.

Our Journey

2020

Custom Wear is founded with the passion to create personalised apparel that tells your story.

2021

Expanded into full custom printing for brands, fitness teams, and lifestyle creators.

2023

Added design services, helping clients create logos, branding, and full apparel concepts.

2025

Serving businesses, creators, and communities nationwide with premium custom apparel.

OUR ORDER PROCESS

STEP 01

Share Your Idea

Send us your design or let our team create one. We’ll review it and send you a quote.

STEP 02

Approve & Pay

Approve the quote and make payment to secure your order.

STEP 03

Production Starts

We begin production and ensure everything meets our quality standards.

Lead Times

We aim to have your order ready within 5 working days, depending on the quantity. Larger orders may require slightly more time, but we’ll always keep you informed throughout the process.

Minimum Orders

We believe everyone should have access to custom apparel, so we don’t require a minimum order. Whether you want a single custom piece or a bulk order, we can accommodate your needs.

Delivery & Collection

Once your order is complete, it will be ready for collection at our studio. If you prefer, we can also arrange delivery directly to your doorstep upon request.
