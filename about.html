About Custom Wear

At Custom Wear, we turn your ideas into reality, one print, one stitch, and one statement at a time. We specialise in personalised apparel that tells your story, whether it’s for your brand, business, or fitness team. From bold streetwear designs to sleek corporate branding and creative gym fits, every piece we create is made to represent you.



Founded with a passion for creativity, quality, and self-expression, Custom Wear was built on the belief that clothing is more than fabric, it’s identity. We combine modern printing techniques with attention to detail, ensuring every logo, quote, and name comes to life with lasting quality and comfort.

Our clients range from local entrepreneurs and fitness brands to wedding parties and lifestyle creators, all trusting us to deliver apparel that stands out, fits right, and feels personal. Whether you need one custom hoodie or a full team collection, we’re here to make it happen, your way.

At Custom Wear, we don’t just print, we create connections. Every design starts with your vision and ends with a piece you’ll be proud to wear.

How we work

Getting your custom wear is simple and straightforward:

1

Reach out with your idea
Send us your ready-to-go design or ask our team to create one for you (note: a small design fee applies). Once we have your design, we’ll provide a quote based on the size, complexity, and colors.

→
2

Approve and make payment
After you approve the quote, we’ll request payment to secure your order.

→
3

Production begins
Once payment is received, we start production and ensure each piece meets our high-quality standards.

Lead Times

We aim to have your order ready within 5 working days, depending on the quantity. Larger orders may require slightly more time, but we’ll always keep you informed throughout the process.

Minimum Orders

We believe everyone should have access to custom apparel, so we don’t require a minimum order. Whether you want a single custom piece or a bulk order, we can accommodate your needs.

Delivery & Collection

Once your order is complete, it will be ready for collection at our studio. If you prefer, we can also arrange delivery directly to your doorstep upon request.
